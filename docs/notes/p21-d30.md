## How the golden P21 files were made
`drivers/vendors/nelko/tspl/test/golden_p21_14x40_d{8,15}.bin` and `golden_p21_14x40.bitmap` (once under `p21/test/`) came
from julienvincent/nelko-p21-rs (MIT). A small Rust harness, outside the repo, called
`NelkoP21::print_bitmap()` on a mock transport and dumped what was written. The bitmap is a border,
a diagonal and a checker pattern of 96×284 dots, packed by the crate's `pack_bitmap`.

The driver's whole job stream matches these files byte for byte (`t_job_golden` in
`drivers/test/test_p21.c`).

D30 has no golden file. Running polskafan's script was refused by the sandbox (it's outside code),
so the D30 test checks against the byte strings documented in the protocol note.

## Contract interpretation (for the hub author)
- Copies: the driver takes either
  - JOB_END(copy) followed straight away by the next copy's FEEDs, or
  - a fresh JOB_BEGIN with the same id.

  Either way it writes a new head per copy. It calls JOB_DONE once, after the last copy.
- FEED returns whole rows, and may take fewer than offered. PEX_EBUSY means retry later; the
  driver also calls PRN_HUB_KICK when room comes back.
- `black_bit` in the caps: P21 is 0 (like the PM220), D30 is 1. The web app must pack per model.
- `orientation: along_feed`: the 96 dots run across the tape, so text along the label is
  rotated 90° in the bitmap.

## Fake host notes
- `drivers/test/Makefile` had no header dependencies, so a change to an included `.inc.c` did
  not rebuild the module. I added `-MMD -MP` and a `-include` of the `.d` files in `modules.mk`.
- `EXTRA_OBJ_$*` in the link rule never expands, because there's no `.SECONDEXPANSION`. Driver
  objects therefore go into `MODS_OBJ`.
- While a peer is stalled, `pex_bt_classic_send` refuses even though `tx_room` reports room. The
  driver handles that: it falls back to holding rows in its own buffer.
